Private Advertising Management Tool

Seller Ads Insight

A private application designed to review, analyze, and optimize advertising performance for Amazon Ads accounts owned or directly managed by the application operator.

The application may use the Amazon Ads API, subject to Amazon approval and account authorization, to retrieve campaign reports, evaluate keywords and bids, monitor advertising costs, and support advertising decisions. It is intended for internal use, not as a public service for third-party sellers.

Independent application. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Amazon.

Information & Data Handling

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This policy describes the intended data handling practices of Seller Ads Insight, a private tool for the application operator's own authorized Amazon advertising accounts.

1. Information accessed

With appropriate authorization, the application may access advertising account identifiers, campaign settings, keywords, bids, budgets, performance reports, and related advertising metrics. This public information page does not request Amazon passwords or collect visitor account information.

2. Purpose of use

Authorized data is used solely for internal advertising reporting, performance analysis, campaign monitoring, and account-owner-approved optimization. The application is not offered as a third-party advertising management service.

3. Access and sharing

Access is limited to the application operator and systems acting on their behalf. Advertising account information is not sold, publicly published, or shared for unrelated marketing purposes. Any service providers used to operate the application should receive only the data necessary for their services, subject to appropriate safeguards.

4. Storage and protection

Advertising data and access credentials should be stored using access controls and appropriate security practices. Secret credentials must not be embedded in this public website or a public code repository. The public page itself does not use tracking cookies, third-party analytics, or a data collection form.

5. Retention and deletion

Data should be retained only as long as necessary for the stated internal functions or applicable legal obligations. The account owner may revoke authorization in Amazon's applicable account settings and may request deletion of locally retained information using the contact address below.

6. Changes

This policy may be revised as the application develops. Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
